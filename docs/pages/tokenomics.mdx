## Community Allocations

### Foundation Treasury

The Interfold has been built in the open and is freely available as open-source software under the
LGPLv3 license. A portion of supply is allocated to The Interfold Foundation. Grants may be awarded
proactively to support priority work or retroactively to recognize meaningful contributions.

### CCA Sale

This allocation was reserved for two Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auctions, intended to give
community members and early believers in The Interfold a fair opportunity to participate before
token transferability took place.

### Airdrop

3.94% of supply was airdropped to web3, privacy and security experts and organizations in order to
recognize contributions to the space. This allocation has already been executed and fully allocated,
and vests over 24 months.

## Other

### Gnosis Guild

This allocation recognises Gnosis Guild's early role and is subject to a 48-month release schedule.

### Investors

The Interfold, previously developed under the name Enclave, has completed the following fundraising
rounds.

- Pre-seed | $20m valuation
- Seed | $25.8m valuation
- Legion round | $25.8m valuation

This allocation combines all three rounds. It has no lock policy, and becomes transferable in full
at the TGE. The Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auctions are shown separately, under the CCA Sale
allocation.

### Team and Advisors

This allocation covers the core team, and the advisors who supported Interfold's early development.
It follows a 24-month release schedule.
