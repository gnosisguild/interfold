# FOLD Transferability

General FOLD transferability is scheduled as part of the Interfold launch sequence on Aug 19.

This guide explains when transferability can be enabled, what changes once it is active, how auction
participants can claim FOLD, and where to find official Interfold resources.

<Callout type='warning'>
  Use only official Interfold links and verified contract addresses. Do not trust unsolicited DMs,
  copied contract addresses, impersonator accounts, or unofficial claiming and trading links.
</Callout>

## When does general FOLD transferability begin?

General FOLD transferability can be enabled at or after:

**Aug 19 at 14:00 UTC (10 AM ET)**

**Unix timestamp:** `1787148000`

At or after that timestamp, anyone can call the `tge()` function on the FOLD contract to enable
general transferability.

The timestamp does not automatically enable transfers by itself. General transferability begins once
the `tge()` transaction has successfully executed onchain.

The function is permissionless, meaning it can be called by anyone once the timestamp has passed. It
does not depend on a specific Interfold-controlled account.

Official Interfold channels will confirm once general transferability has been enabled.

## What changes once general transferability is enabled?

Once general FOLD transferability is enabled:

- FOLD can be transferred between addresses without the launch-period general transfer restriction.
- Holders can interact with FOLD through supported onchain interfaces.
- FOLD can be used for supported network functions, including ciphernode bonding where available.
- Allocation-specific vesting, lockups, or contractual restrictions continue to apply where
  relevant.

General FOLD transferability does not override restrictions that apply to a specific FOLD
allocation.

## Is FOLD transferability the same as Network Alpha?

No. General FOLD transferability and Network Alpha are distinct milestones within the broader
Interfold launch sequence.

**General FOLD transferability** changes the transfer state of FOLD.

**Network Alpha** is the first mainnet operating phase of the Interfold network.

For more on FOLD and its role in the network, see:

- [FOLD Token](/faq/fold-token)
- [FOLD Tokenomics](/tokenomics)

## I participated in the FOLD auction. When can I claim?

Claiming for FOLD Auction 2 is expected to open immediately after the auction closes on Aug 19 at
**13:00 UTC (9 AM ET)**.

Allocated FOLD and any unspent USDC can then be claimed through the verified auction interface.

Claiming and general FOLD transferability are separate actions. Auction 2 closes one hour before
general FOLD transferability can be enabled.

Start from the official [FOLD Auction page](https://www.theinterfold.com/fold-auction).

For current auction and claiming information, see the [FOLD Auction FAQ](/faq/auction).

## Do I need to do anything to FOLD I already hold?

General FOLD transferability does not itself require holders to claim through the Auction 2
interface.

Any vesting, lockup, or contractual restrictions applicable to a specific allocation continue to be
governed by the terms of that allocation.

For currently published allocation and unlock information, see [FOLD Tokenomics](/tokenomics).

## Can FOLD be used for ciphernode bonding?

FOLD supports ciphernode bonding as part of the economic operation of the Interfold network.

Ciphernode operators bond FOLD to become eligible for network duties and economically accountable
for correct participation.

For current operator requirements, setup instructions, and bonding information, see:

- [Ciphernode Operators](/ciphernode-operators)
- [Running a Ciphernode](/ciphernode-operators/running)
- [Registration & Bonding](/ciphernode-operators/registration)

Current operator documentation is the source of truth for network participation requirements and
bonding mechanics.

## Does general transferability remove vesting or lockups?

No. General transferability does not override vesting schedules, contractual restrictions, or other
conditions that apply to specific FOLD allocations.

See [FOLD Tokenomics](/tokenomics) for currently published allocation and unlock information.

## How do I stay safe around FOLD transferability?

Periods around token transferability can attract impersonators and fraudulent links.

Before interacting with FOLD:

- Start from an official Interfold website, documentation page, or verified social account.
- Verify the FOLD contract address before signing a transaction.
- Do not trust contract addresses, claiming links, or trading links sent through unsolicited DMs.
- Do not send assets to anyone offering manual claiming, migration, unlocking, or support.
- Never share your seed phrase, private key, or wallet backup.
- Review the connected network, destination, and transaction details before signing.

Interfold team members and moderators will never ask for your seed phrase, private key, wallet
backup, or access to your wallet.
