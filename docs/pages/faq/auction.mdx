## What is a Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction?

A Continuous Clearing Auction (CCA) is a price-discovery mechanism for token distribution built on
Uniswap infrastructure.

- The auction runs across multiple discrete periods.
- Participants submit either a market order or a capped order that sets the maximum price they are
  willing to pay.
- At the end of each period, all successful participants pay the same clearing price.
- In the auction, the minimum price for each subsequent period is the greater of the original floor
  price or the previous period’s clearing price. As a result, the minimum price may rise in later
  auction periods, but it does not fall from one period to the next.
- Any tokens not distributed earlier remain available in the final period.

By distributing tokens progressively and applying a uniform price within each period, the CCA is
designed to reduce gas competition, mitigate sniping, and allow clearing prices to develop through
participant demand. The result is intended to support a more transparent and orderly auction process
for participants.

[Read more about Uniswap CCAs](https://cca.uniswap.org).

## Who could participate in the FOLD auction?

Participation in the FOLD auction was available to eligible participants, subject to jurisdiction,
identity verification, AML and sanctions screening, and other applicable restrictions.

Additional restrictions could apply based on jurisdiction, residence, citizenship, entity status,
sanctions screening, or other legal requirements. Participation could be restricted in certain
jurisdictions, including the UK. EU participation could also be subject to limits of fewer than 149
persons per member state.

Eligibility was determined through the official verification process and governed by the auction
terms.

## Did participants need to complete KYC?

Yes. Participants were required to complete identity verification through Predicate, Interfold’s
third-party verification provider, before participating in the auction.

The verification process was used to confirm jurisdictional eligibility and conduct AML and
sanctions screening in accordance with the auction requirements.

Completing verification did not guarantee eligibility or an allocation. Participation remained
subject to the auction terms and applicable restrictions.

## Is there any cooldown period associated with FOLD purchased during the FOLD token auction?

Yes. Tokens purchased through the FOLD auction are subject to a 40-day cooldown. During this period,
general transfers are restricted.

The FOLD token contract permits FOLD to be used for ciphernode bonding during the cooldown. The
ciphernode operator process is not live yet; technical requirements, bonding instructions, and
operator launch details will be published separately through official Interfold channels.

TGE and general FOLD transferability are scheduled for August 19, following the 40-day cooldown.

## When did the FOLD auction take place?

Registration and verification opened July 6. The live bidding window ran for 48 hours, from July 8
at 10am ET through July 10 at 10am ET.

The auction has closed. For current participant instructions, use the
[FOLD Post-Auction FAQ](https://blog.theinterfold.com/fold-post-auction-faq/).
