## What is FOLD Auction 2?

FOLD Auction 2 is the second public FOLD auction, run using Uniswap’s Continuous Clearing Auction
mechanism.

It follows the initial FOLD Auction held July 8–10, 2026.

Auction 2 is intentionally limited to **2% of total FOLD supply** and is being held around the
transition to general FOLD transferability to broaden distribution and help bootstrap deeper,
longer-term onchain liquidity.

## Why is there a second FOLD Auction?

Auction 2 is designed as a limited distribution step ahead of general FOLD transferability on
Aug 19.

It is intentionally small at **2% of total FOLD supply** and begins with a floor matching the
clearing price of the first FOLD Auction.

The auction provides a transparent, public mechanism for additional distribution before FOLD becomes
generally transferable, while supporting long-term onchain liquidity.

**50% of proceeds will be dedicated to long-term onchain liquidity support.**

Auction 2 closes shortly before general FOLD transferability can be enabled.

## What is a Uniswap Continuous Clearing Auction?

A Continuous Clearing Auction (CCA) is a price-discovery mechanism for token distribution built on
Uniswap infrastructure.

- The auction runs across multiple discrete periods.
- Participants submit bids through the official Uniswap CCA interface.
- Successful bids within a period clear at the same clearing price.
- The minimum price for each subsequent period is the greater of the original floor price or the
  previous period’s clearing price.
- The minimum price may rise in later periods, but does not fall below the applicable prior clearing
  price or original floor.
- Any tokens not distributed earlier remain available in the final period.

By distributing tokens progressively and applying a uniform price within each period, the CCA is
designed to reduce gas competition, mitigate sniping, and allow clearing prices to develop through
auction participation.

[Read more about Uniswap CCAs](https://cca.uniswap.org).

## When can participants claim FOLD?

Claiming is expected to open immediately after the auction closes.

Allocated FOLD and any unspent USDC can then be claimed through the verified auction interface.

Claiming and general FOLD transferability are separate actions.

## When does general FOLD transferability begin?

FOLD Auction 2 closes Aug 19 at **13:06 UTC (9:06 AM ET)**.

General FOLD transferability can be enabled shortly afterward, at or after **14:00 UTC (10 AM ET)**.

At or after Unix timestamp **`1787148000`**, anyone can call the `tge()` function on the FOLD
contract to enable general transferability.

Once that transaction is executed, FOLD becomes generally transferable.

FOLD Auction 2, claiming, general FOLD transferability, and Network Alpha are distinct milestones
within the broader Interfold launch sequence.

## Does the auction determine the price of FOLD after transferability?

No. The auction determines allocations and a clearing price within the auction itself. It does not
determine FOLD’s market price once general transferability begins.

Future market prices cannot be predicted.
